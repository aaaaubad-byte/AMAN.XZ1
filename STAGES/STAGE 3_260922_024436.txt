13. Number Ownership

Every phone number must belong to the authenticated customer.

The relationship must effectively enforce:

customer_numbers.customer_id = authenticated customer 

Use the Stage 2 RLS/RPC security model.

Do not rely on the UI to hide other customers' numbers.

A phone number being registered/added does not automatically mean that it is protected.

The customer can add multiple numbers and later submit a protection request for an eligible number.

15. New Protection Request Flow

The flow must be:

Step 1 — Select Number

Display only phone numbers belonging to the current customer.

Step 2 — Select Protection Plan

Display only plans associated with the telecom provider of the selected number.

The customer must not see plans belonging to unrelated providers.

Step 3 — Display Price

Display the real price returned by the database/backend.

Do NOT hardcode prices.

Step 4 — Select Payment Method

Display currently available customer-facing payment methods.

Step 5 — Payment Instructions

Display the payment instructions/details supplied by the actual backend contract.

Step 6 — Enter Payment Information

Display the required payment-reference/transfer fields defined by the Stage 2 contract.

Step 7 — Review

Display a complete review summary:

• Phone number.

• Telecom provider.

• Protection plan.

• Price.

• Payment method.

• Payment information.

Step 8 — Submit

On confirmation, call the secure protection-request RPC/backend contract implemented during Stage 2.

The Android client must NOT directly create a protection.
